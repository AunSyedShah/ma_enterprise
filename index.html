    How a repair works

    Four steps from the first call to a working kitchen

    1. Step 1

      Call or book online

      Tell us the equipment and the symptom. We tell you when a technician can reach you.

    2. Step 2

      Diagnosis on site

      The technician tests pressure, temperature and electrical supply and finds the root cause.

    3. Step 3

      Quote before work

      You see the cost of parts and labour first. Nothing is replaced without your yes.

    4. Step 4

      Repair and test

      We fix it, run the unit until it reaches setpoint and check back after a few days.
